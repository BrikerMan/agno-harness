# Leave

## Annual leave

- 1 year of service: 5 days
- 3 years of service: 10 days
- 10 years of service: 15 days
- Annual leave follows the calendar year. Unused days do not carry over.
- Request it in a private Teams chat with the HR assistant. Include the start date, end date, and the person covering the work. Ask at least 3 working days ahead.

## Sick leave

- One day or less: tell the manager in Teams the same day.
- More than one day: send a hospital note to HR.
- Sick leave does not use annual leave.
